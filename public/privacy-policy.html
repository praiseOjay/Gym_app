Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • Last Updated: September 16, 2026

At a Glance: Our Privacy Commitment

  • 100% Offline-First Storage: Your workout logs, sets, reps, and volume history remain on your personal device (IndexedDB). We do not collect or sell your fitness history.
  • Ephemeral Voice Audio: Spoken sets and reps are transcribed in real-time. Audio is immediately discarded and never recorded or stored.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, lease, or monetize your training data to third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

A. Local Device Fitness Data

When you log sets, calculate 1RM records, or build custom training mesocycles, all entries are stored locally on your device via IndexedDB. We do not store this data on our servers.

B. Microphone Permissions & Voice Logger

Overload AI offers an optional hands-free audio logger for training sessions. The app only accesses your microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) when you actively tap the voice entry button. Voice audio is processed ephemerally in real-time solely to identify numbers and exercises, and is immediately discarded. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to external databases.

C. AI Coach Intelligence (Google Gemini)

When you chat with Coach Overload or request a Smart Exercise Swap, your prompt and non-identifying exercise context are securely forwarded via our cloud proxy to the Google Gemini API. We never send your name, email, or personal identity.

D. Subscriptions & Payments

Purchases of Overload Pro subscriptions are processed securely through the Google Play Store via Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or payment credentials.

2. Data Control & Deletion

You have total control over your data. You can delete individual workouts or trigger a complete factory reset at any time inside the app by going to Settings > Factory Reset / Clear Data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local records.

3. Health & Medical Disclaimer

Notice: Overload AI provides progressive overload calculations and fitness guidance for educational and self-tracking purposes only. It is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a physician before beginning any exercise routine.
